# EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT NOTE

**Chief complaint:** Right shoulder injury

**HPI**

34 y/o male, presents after a fall onto the outstretched right arm. Pain and weakness lifting the arm overhead. Sleeping on that side is difficult.

**PMH:** Nil of note
**Meds:** None
**Allergies:** None known.

Right shoulder: limited active abduction, painful arc. External rotation weaker than the other side. Neurovascularly intact distally.

**Imaging**

**Assessment / Plan**
