# EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT NOTE

**Chief complaint:** Left shoulder injury

**HPI**

46 y/o male, presents after catching a heavy object as it slipped, with a sudden jolt to the left shoulder. Pain and weakness lifting the arm overhead. Sleeping on that side is difficult.

**PMH:** Nil of note
**Meds:** None
**Allergies:** None known.

**Examination**

Left shoulder: limited active abduction, painful arc. External rotation weaker than the other side. Neurovascularly intact distally.

**Imaging**

> Radiologist read: Left shoulder, three views. No acute fracture. Acromial morphology unremarkable. Soft tissues non-diagnostic for cuff integrity.

**Assessment / Plan**

Sling for comfort. Analgesia. Referral to physiotherapy and to orthopaedics.
