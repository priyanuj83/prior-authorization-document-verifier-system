**Chief complaint:** Right shoulder injury

**HPI**

6 y/o male, presents after catching a heavy object as it slipped, with a sudden jolt to the right shoulder. Pain and weakness lifting the arm overhead. Sleeping on that side is difficult.

**PMH:** Nil of note
**Meds:** None
**Allergies:** None known.

Right shoulder: limited active abduction, painful arc. External rotation weaker than the other side. Neurovascularly intact distally.

**Imaging**

**Assessment / Plan**

Sling for comfort. Analgesia. Referral to physiotherapy and to orthopaedics.
