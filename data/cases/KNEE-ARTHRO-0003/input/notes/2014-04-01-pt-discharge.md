# PHYSIOTHERAPY DISCHARGE SUMMARY

**Referral source:** Emergency Department. Referral received on the date of her
attendance there following the kerb incident, and she was booked in from that point.

**Body region treated:** Left knee.

**Course of treatment**

Mrs Romaguera attended for initial assessment here in the department shortly after the
referral came through and has been seen **twice weekly** since. She completed **15
attended sessions** in total. Attendance was reliable; she missed two appointments,
both due to transport difficulties, and both were rescheduled.

Every session was delivered here in the outpatient department with hands-on
assessment and supervised progression at each visit. She was given a small home
programme to maintain between visits, which she reports doing, but the substance of
the treatment was the supervised work here.

Programme content:

- Effusion and pain management, including manual therapy to the peri-articular soft
  tissues.
- Quadriceps and hip abductor strengthening, progressed as tolerated within the
  limits of her fixed flexion deformity.
- Range of motion work, with realistic goals given the established degenerative
  changes.
- Gait re-education with her stick, and balance work.

**Outcome**

Some genuine gains. Her quadriceps strength has improved measurably and her walking
tolerance has increased from around 200 metres to closer to 400 metres. Her baseline
arthritic pain is somewhat better controlled than at the start.

But the **mechanical catching that prompted the referral has not changed at all.**
She reported catching episodes in most weeks of the course, including the final week.
Attempts to progress rotational loading were consistently limited by the catching
rather than by pain, strength, or her arthritis. On two occasions the catching
occurred during a session here.

Her range of motion remains 10 to 100 degrees. I would not expect this to improve
further given the structural state of the joint.

**Discharge status**

Discharged today having completed the course as requested by Mr Castellanos. In my
view she has extracted what benefit is available from therapy. The catching is
mechanical and structural and will not respond to further rehabilitation.

Summary copied to Mr Castellanos as requested.
