# EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT NOTE

**Chief complaint:** Right knee injury

**HPI**

33 y/o male, presents after a stumble on uneven ground with a twist of the right knee. Immediate pain on the inner side of the right knee. Unable to continue the activity. Weight bearing is painful.

**PMH:** Nil of note
**Meds:** None
**Allergies:** None known.

**Examination**

Right knee: moderate effusion. Tenderness along the medial joint line. Range limited by pain. Ligament testing limited by guarding; no gross laxity appreciated. Neurovascularly intact distally.

**Imaging**

> Radiologist read: Right knee, three views. No acute fracture or dislocation. Minimal marginal changes only, joint spaces preserved. Kellgren-Lawrence grade 1. The menisci are not assessable on these films.

**Assessment / Plan**

Analgesia. Crutches as needed. Referral placed today to the outpatient physiotherapy service for the right knee. Orthopaedic follow-up arranged.
