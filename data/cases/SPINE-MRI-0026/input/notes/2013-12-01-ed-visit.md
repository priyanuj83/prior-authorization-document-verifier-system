# EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT NOTE

**Chief complaint:** Neck pain after collision

**HPI**

65 y/o female, presents after being struck from behind while stopped at a junction. Neck pain began immediately. No loss of consciousness. Unable to turn the head comfortably.

**PMH:** Nil of note
**Meds:** None
**Allergies:** None known.

Alert, cervical collar in place. Midline neck tenderness. No step-off. Upper limb power appears intact at the bedside though limited by pain. No obvious distal neurological deficit on a brief exam.

Cervical spine films obtained. Orthopaedic/spine discussion. Advanced imaging of the cervical spine requested.
