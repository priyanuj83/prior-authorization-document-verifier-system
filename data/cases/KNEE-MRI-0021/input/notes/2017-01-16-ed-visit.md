# EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT NOTE

**Chief complaint:** Left knee injury

**HPI**

23 y/o female, presents after coming down awkwardly from a jump during a gym class. Immediate pain on the inner side of the left knee. Unable to continue the activity. Weight bearing is painful.

**PMH:** Nil of note
**Meds:** None
**Allergies:** None known.

**Examination**

Left knee: moderate effusion. Tenderness along the medial joint line. Range limited by pain. Ligament testing limited by guarding; no gross laxity appreciated. Neurovascularly intact distally.

**Imaging**

> Radiologist read: Left knee, three views. No acute fracture or dislocation. Minimal marginal changes only, joint spaces preserved. Kellgren-Lawrence grade 1. The menisci are not assessable on these films.

**Assessment / Plan**

Analgesia. Crutches as needed. Referral placed today to the outpatient physiotherapy service for the left knee. Orthopaedic follow-up arranged.
