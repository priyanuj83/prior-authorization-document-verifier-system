# MRI LEFT KNEE WITHOUT CONTRAST

**Clinical indication:** Persistent mechanical symptoms following twisting injury
January 2014. Query meniscal tear. Referred by Dr R. Danvers.

**Technique:** Multiplanar multisequence imaging of the left knee at 1.5T. Sagittal,
coronal and axial proton density and fat-suppressed sequences obtained. No contrast
administered.

**Comparison:** Plain radiographs left knee 2014-01-31.

## Findings

**Menisci.** There is an oblique tear through the posterior horn of the medial
meniscus, extending from the free edge to the inferior articular surface. The tear
measures approximately 14 mm in its longest dimension. The meniscal fragment is
mildly mobile on sequential images but remains within the meniscal substance —
**there is no displaced fragment and no bucket-handle configuration.** The meniscal
root attachments are intact both anteriorly and posteriorly. The lateral meniscus is
normal in size, shape and signal throughout.

**Cruciate ligaments.** Anterior cruciate ligament intact with normal fibre
orientation and signal. Posterior cruciate ligament intact.

**Collateral ligaments.** Medial collateral ligament intact. Lateral collateral
ligament complex intact.

**Articular cartilage.** Grade 1 chondral signal change over the weight-bearing
surface of the medial femoral condyle, without fissuring or full-thickness loss.
Cartilage elsewhere is preserved. No subchondral marrow oedema. No osteophytosis.

**Extensor mechanism.** Quadriceps and patellar tendons intact. Patellofemoral
cartilage normal. Patellar tracking normal.

**Other.** Trace joint effusion. No loose body. No popliteal cyst. Bone marrow signal
normal throughout. No fracture.

## Impression

1. **Oblique tear of the posterior horn of the medial meniscus** extending to the
   inferior articular surface. No displaced or bucket-handle fragment.
2. Minimal grade 1 chondral change of the medial femoral condyle. No significant
   degenerative disease.
3. Cruciate and collateral ligaments intact.
