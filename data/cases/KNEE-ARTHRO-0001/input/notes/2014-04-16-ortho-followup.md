# ORTHOPAEDIC FOLLOW-UP

**Interval history**

Reviewed following completion of his therapy course and the MRI I requested.

He finished with the physiotherapy department at the beginning of this month. I have
their discharge summary. They confirm he attended consistently and completed the full
programme they set out, but his mechanical symptoms did not settle at any point during
it — indeed the knee locked twice during sessions in their department.

Since discharge from therapy a fortnight ago the picture is unchanged. He reports two
further locking episodes in the last week, both requiring him to straighten the leg by
hand.

**Examination**

Left knee: no effusion now. Full extension, flexion 130 degrees. Quadriceps bulk
symmetrical, consistent with the strength gains therapy achieved.

Posteromedial joint line tenderness persists. McMurray's remains positive with a
reproducible click. Ligaments stable throughout.

**Imaging review**

MRI of 9 April confirms an oblique tear of the posterior horn of the medial meniscus
extending to the inferior articular surface, with intact cruciates and only grade 1
chondral change medially. No displaced fragment.

Plain films from January showed preserved joint spaces, Kellgren-Lawrence grade 1.

**Assessment**

Symptomatic medial meniscal tear in a 33-year-old with a structurally sound,
non-arthritic knee. His symptoms are mechanical and reproducible, they are corroborated
on imaging, and they have persisted unchanged through and beyond a full supervised
therapy programme that did succeed in restoring his strength. There is no meaningful
degenerative disease to argue against intervention.

**Plan**

1. Left knee arthroscopy with partial medial meniscectomy. Repair will be assessed
   intraoperatively but given the tear configuration and location, resection is the
   likely course.
2. Risks discussed including infection, thromboembolism, stiffness, incomplete relief
   of symptoms, and the possibility of later degenerative change. He understands and
   wishes to proceed.
3. Authorisation request to be submitted to his plan today. Therapy discharge summary
   and MRI report to accompany the submission.
4. Listed for day-case surgery once authorisation is received.
